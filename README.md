# Journey Journal — NYC Walks & Rides (companion notes)

**Journey Journal** is a YouTube channel of **real-time 4K walking and cycling
videos of New York City with pure ambient sound** — no commentary, no added
music on the main films. Every video is a continuous journey through a real
neighborhood: Harlem, Midtown Manhattan, Flushing, Astoria, Randall's Island,
and beyond.

▶ Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@journeyjournal-q

This repository is the **route & history companion**: one page per journey with
the exact route, the landmarks on camera (with verified dates and facts), and
practical FAQ (how to get there, what you'll see, what you'll hear). Pages are
published at: https://wenchangyue.github.io/journey-journal-nyc/

## What makes these videos different
- **Real time, real sound** — original street/park ambience, recorded on
  DJI Osmo Pocket 3 and GoPro in 4K.
- **Verified on-screen history** — bridges, stadiums and parks are labeled in
  the film with dates cross-checked against primary sources.
- **Full routes** — start point to end point, chapter by chapter, so you can
  follow the same walk or ride yourself.
