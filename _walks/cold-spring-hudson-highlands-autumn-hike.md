## The journey

The hike follows a rocky trail through a mixed deciduous forest. Early sections
cross sun-dappled dirt paths and stone ledges; the middle of the hike climbs
through a **boulder field** where yellow and orange leaves frame a deep blue
sky. Later, a group of hikers moves ahead up short **scrambles** over rocks and
fallen logs, and the final stretch continues through golden woods.

The trail name is not given because the footage has no GPS track and no trail
sign is clearly readable on screen.

## The Hudson Highlands

According to New York State Parks, **Hudson Highlands State Park Preserve** is a
mostly undeveloped preserve of **over 8,000 acres** with **over 70 miles of
trail**, stretching from Annsville Creek in Peekskill north to Dennings Point
in Beacon, about **50 miles north of New York City**. The trail network ranges
from easy to challenging, and the agency notes that weekends are very busy and
parking is limited.
