## The journey

The walk begins on residential streets in **Hamilton Heights**, following the
rise beside the City College campus. Trees, brownstones, apartment buildings,
and the steep Manhattan terrain frame the approach. The route reaches the
modern science complex on **St. Nicholas Terrace**, where signs identify the
Advanced Science Research Center and the City College Center for Discovery and
Innovation.

From the science campus, the camera continues toward the **North Academic
Center** and the main CCNY grounds. The middle of the walk contrasts modern
academic buildings with the stone towers and detailed façades of the historic
campus. The route then follows the campus edge near **Convent Avenue** before
looping through the surrounding Harlem and Hamilton Heights streets.

The chapter labels stay broad because the footage does not provide a complete
GPS track for every turn. Building and street names are used only where signs,
the official CCNY map, or the campus address list support them.

## The campus

City College was founded in **1847** as the Free Academy. CCNY's official
history says the college moved uptown in **1907** to the Neo-Gothic campus
designed by **George Browne Post**. The original buildings around the North
Campus Quad are listed on national, state, and New York City historic
registers. CCNY's fact sheet describes the campus as extending along Convent
Avenue between West 130th and West 141st Streets.

The modern science complex seen from St. Nicholas Terrace includes the
**Advanced Science Research Center at the CUNY Graduate Center**. Its official
address is **85 Saint Nicholas Terrace**, on the south end of the City College
campus.
