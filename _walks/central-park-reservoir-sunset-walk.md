## The journey

The walk begins on leaf-covered paths in low afternoon light, then crosses
open lawns as the sun drops behind the trees. After a stretch along a park
drive with cyclists and runners, the route reaches the **Jacqueline Kennedy
Onassis Reservoir** just as the sun sets behind the Upper West Side skyline.

The camera then follows the fenced **running track** beside deep orange
trees, before turning back into quieter autumn paths in the blue light after
sunset and ending near the edge of the park.

Path names are kept general because the footage has no GPS track; only
landmarks clearly visible on screen are named.

## The Reservoir

According to the Central Park Conservancy, the Reservoir is the **largest water
body in Central Park** and one of its most popular places to run. It was
constructed between **1858 and 1862**, decommissioned in **1993**, and named for
**Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis** in **1994** in honor of her contributions to the
city. Central Park itself covers **843 acres** and was designed by Frederick
Law Olmsted and Calvert Vaux.
