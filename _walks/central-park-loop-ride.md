## The journey

The ride begins three blocks from the park, on **West 113th Street** in
South Harlem, in full golden-hour light. At **Central Park North (110th
Street)** it rolls onto the park drives — completely **car-free since
June 27, 2018** — and joins the counterclockwise loop that riders,
runners and carriages share.

The first act heads south on the **West Drive**: past the woods and
lawns of the park's quieter upper half, with the twin towers of the
**El Dorado** (1931) rising over the trees at 90th Street, joggers and
Citi Bikes streaming past, and the first supertalls of **Billionaires'
Row** appearing above the canopy as Midtown gets closer.

Near the south end of the park, the ride meets its oldest tradition:
**horse-drawn carriages**, queued at 59th Street since 1862. A
red-canopied carriage with a white horse rolls past close enough to
hear the hooves — the moment that opens the video.

Then the loop turns north up the **East Drive** along the Fifth Avenue
side, and the light changes: sunset came at 8:12 pm this evening, a
brief shower slicked the asphalt, and the blue hour settles over the
park as the lamps come on. The last miles climb the quiet northern
hills in deepening dusk, ending back at **Central Park North** — one
full loop, about **6.1 miles (10 km)**, golden hour to dark.

Real park sound throughout — hooves, wheels, birds and the evening
crowd — with a **soft instrumental music bed** mixed well beneath the
ambience. No narration.
