## The journey

The video opens under the inflatable entrance arch for **潮玩中秋·好市集**, the
2026 Mid-Autumn Market in downtown **Flushing, Queens**. From the Main Street
end, the camera moves south along **Kissena Boulevard** between white tents,
food smoke, produce displays, clothing and craft tables, and community
information booths. The wide street remains open to pedestrians for the event,
with the storefronts and apartment buildings of downtown Flushing framing the
market.

Near the **Chinese-American Supermarket**, the walk passes a denser run of food
and retail tents before reaching **Barclay Avenue**. Farther south, the route
moves through activity and service booths beside the EmblemHealth/AdvantageCare
building. At **Sanford Avenue**, the bus shelter reads `Kissena Blvd & Sanford
Avenue`; the camera crosses the edge of the market, then turns north for a
partial return through the final vendor rows.

## The event

World Journal reported that the market ran from **10 AM to 5 PM on Saturday,
September 19, 2026**, along Kissena Boulevard from Main Street to Sanford
Avenue. World Journal organized it with **Flushing BID** and **Asian American
Activity Center, Inc. (AAAC)**. Its same-day report described nearly 70 stalls,
while advance coverage listed food, clothing and household vendors, community
information booths, and live activities. Those categories match what is visible
in the recording; individual booth names are left out unless their signs are
clear.

## Light and sound

The footage was recorded on Saturday afternoon in sun and passing clouds. The
video retains the camera's native **4K HLG HDR at 59.94 fps**. The soundtrack is
the original public-market ambience: crowd movement, food preparation, street
noise, and event announcements. No music or commentary was added, and private
conversation is not transcribed or captioned.
