## The journey

A 93-minute continuous ride across northern **Queens**: starting among the
Chinese storefronts of **Downtown Flushing**, past **Citi Field** (home of the
New York Mets, opened 2009 beside the old Shea Stadium site), along the
**Flushing Bay Promenade** (now the Malcolm X Promenade), through neighborhood
streets to **Astoria**, then up the walkway of the **Robert F. Kennedy Bridge**.
The Triborough Bridge complex opened July 11, 1936; this ride crossed it exactly
90 years later and continued down to **Randall's Island**.

This is Part 1 of a two-part day; Part 2 continues around Randall's Island
itself.
