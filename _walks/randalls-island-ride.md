## The journey

A 52-minute continuous ride around **Randall's Island**, the green island in the
East River where Manhattan, Queens and the Bronx meet. The route follows the
Bronx Kill shoreline, passes under the century-old stone arches of the **Hell
Gate Bridge** (completed 1916 — once the world's longest steel arch, and the
design model for the Sydney Harbour Bridge), loops the island's ballfields to
**Icahn Stadium** (2005), and returns along the Harlem River with the Manhattan
skyline across the water.

The ride was recorded on **July 11, 2026 — exactly 90 years** after President
Franklin D. Roosevelt opened the **Triborough (Robert F. Kennedy) Bridge** on
July 11, 1936. That same day in 1936, **Jesse Owens** won at the final U.S.
Olympic Track & Field Trials held on Randall's Island, on his way to the Berlin
Games; Icahn Stadium now stands on the site of that stadium.

The island's story runs deeper: the Lenape called it **Minnahanonck**; Jonathan
Randel bought it in 1784; New York City purchased it from his heirs for $60,000
in 1835. Today Randall's Island Park has **60+ athletic fields** and waterfront
greenways connecting all three boroughs.
