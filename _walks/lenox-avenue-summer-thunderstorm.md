## The journey

A midday thunderstorm empties the sidewalks of **Lenox Avenue (Malcolm X
Boulevard)** and turns Harlem's main boulevard into a field of grey water and
umbrellas. The walk starts beside the balconied towers of **Lenox Terrace**
(W 132nd–135th Streets), heads south past the hardware stores and delis of the
West 130s — **BLG Hardware** at 439 Lenox among them — and ducks under the
scaffolding at **Fusion Nails (353 Lenox)** between W 127th and 128th.

At **West 125th Street** the walk reaches its southern turn at one of Harlem's
best-known corners: the 310 Lenox block shared by **Red Rooster Harlem** and
the classical white-stone **M&T Bank** building. From there it doubles back
north through the heaviest rain — articulated buses spraying past, yellow cabs
at the **Habibi Halal Deli** corner — to the **Food Dynasty** block at W
132nd–133rd, before turning west at the corner deli at 477 Malcolm X Blvd onto
brownstone-lined **West 134th Street**.

The camera keeps its original HLG HDR picture. The soundtrack is the storm
itself — private conversations removed, the rain gently reinforced with a
layered rain-and-thunder bed, and a soft original piano underneath.
