## The journey

This walk begins among the wet paths and mature trees surrounding **East
Meadow**, an open lawn on Central Park's east side between 97th and 100th
Streets. Heavy summer rain softens the distant view, darkens the pavement, and
brings out reflections under the park lamps and benches. The video keeps the
camera's original HLG HDR picture and the natural sound of rain, footsteps, and
the city beyond the park.

The **Central Park Conservancy** describes East Meadow as one of the Park's
three major meadows. The area was originally intended to form part of an
arboretum that was never built, and it still contains some of Central Park's
prized American elms. The Conservancy restored the meadow in 2010. The broader
park covers 843 acres and was designed by **Frederick Law Olmsted and Calvert
Vaux** as a sequence of lawns, woodlands, streams, lakes, and winding paths.

After the meadow paths, the route reaches **Fifth Avenue** opposite **The Mount
Sinai Hospital**. Mount Sinai's official directions place its Upper East Side
campus between East 98th and East 102nd Streets, from Fifth to Madison Avenue.
The final minutes continue south in the rain to the visible **Museum Mile** and
**East 96th Street** signs, near Central Park's East 96th Street entrance.
