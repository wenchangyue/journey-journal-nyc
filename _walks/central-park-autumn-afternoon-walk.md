## The journey

The walk starts on curving asphalt paths beside **red maples** and the grey
**rock outcrops** that are typical of Central Park's landscape. It crosses one
of the park drives at a striped crosswalk, with cyclists and runners passing,
then moves into quieter woodland paths and open lawns.

Midway, the camera passes the edge of **ballfields** framed by tall trees, then
follows a fenced, tree-lined promenade in low sun. The final stretch looks
across a wide meadow in late-afternoon light.

Path and landmark names are kept general because the footage does not include
a GPS track; only what is visible on screen is described.

## The park

The Central Park Conservancy states that Central Park covers **843 acres** and
was created in **1858**. It was designed by **Frederick Law Olmsted and Calvert
Vaux**, whose plan combined sweeping lawns, woodlands, streams and lakes linked
by winding paths. The Conservancy reports about **42 million visits** a year.
