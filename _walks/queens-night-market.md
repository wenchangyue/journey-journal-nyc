## The journey

Every summer Saturday after dark, the field behind the **New York Hall of
Science** — one of the last buildings standing from the **1964 World's
Fair** — fills with blue tents, grill smoke, and one of the most
international crowds in New York. This is the **Queens Night Market**,
founded in 2015 on a simple idea: cap most dishes at **$5–6** so anyone
can eat their way around the world.

This walk was recorded on **July 25, 2026**, between about 9:15 and 9:25
pm, deep in the market's Saturday rhythm. The camera drifts past the
vendor rows — **Hong Kong egg waffles**, **Brazilian barbecue**, **fresh
sugarcane juice**, cachapas, satay and a hundred other stalls — through
the **picnic grounds** where thousands eat at long tables under
floodlights, and back along dessert row as the smoke rolls over the
tents.

The 2026 season — the market's eleventh — features **over 100 vendors**,
at most two per cuisine, with food representing **more than 100 countries
and cultures**, and Saturday crowds around **20,000** strong. Admission
is free; the market runs Saturdays 4 pm–midnight, April through October,
in **Flushing Meadows Corona Park**, Corona, Queens.

Original market sound throughout — no narration, no music. Just the
crowd, the grills, and a New York night.
