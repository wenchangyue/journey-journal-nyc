## The journey

This walk covers thirty blocks of **Madison Avenue** in one continuous
northbound line, from **East 100th Street** to **East 130th Street**, in the
last hour of evening light on July 17, 2026. The camera keeps its original HLG
HDR picture and the street's real sound — no narration, no music.

The first stretch runs beside the **George Washington Carver Houses**, whose
grounds front Madison Avenue from East 99th to 106th Street, then continues
past school blocks and corner delis into the heart of East Harlem. Around East
112th Street the towers of the **Taft Houses** (dedicated 1961) rise on both
sides of the avenue.

From 120th to 124th Street the iron fence of **Marcus Garvey Park** follows
the west side of the avenue — the park's east edge is Madison Avenue itself.
At **East 125th Street**, Harlem's main crosstown corridor, articulated MTA
buses sweep through the wide intersection; the Harlem–125th Street Metro-North
station stands one block east at Park Avenue.

The architectural climax comes at **East 129th Street**: the golden sandstone
facade of **All Saints Church** (1883–86), "the St. Patrick's of Harlem," lit
directly by the setting sun. The final blocks walk into that light — long
shadows, lens flare between the trees, and the sun dropping to street level
toward East 130th Street.
